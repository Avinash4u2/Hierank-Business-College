Hierank Business School

Hierank Business School, Noida is an integrated campus approved by AICTE under the Ministry of HRD, Govt. of India. The MBA program is affiliated with Dr. Abdul Kalam Technical University (formerly UPTU), Lucknow, and the graduate programs (BBA, BCA/B.Ed) are affiliated with CCS University Meerut, under UGC..

OUR PROGRAMMES

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

AICTE-approved and affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow (College Code: 733).

Undergraduate Programs (UG)

The BBA and BCA programs are affiliated with CCS University (Code: 879) and approved by the UGC under the Ministry of HRD.

Our Faculties

Our institution is supported by a team of highly qualified and experienced faculty members. They focus on delivering practical knowledge along with strong academic foundations. Through mentorship and guidance, they help students grow both professionally and personally..

Placement 2024-25

We provide excellent placement opportunities with reputed companies across various industries. Our dedicated placement cell conducts training sessions, mock interviews, and skill development programs to prepare students for successful careers..

Computer Lab

Our computer labs are equipped with modern systems and high-speed internet to support hands-on learning. Students get practical exposure to the latest software and technologies, enhancing their technical and problem-solving skills.

Library

Our college library is a hub of learning, designed to support students in their academic and personal development. It offers a peaceful and resource-rich environment where students can explore a wide range of books, journals, and digital materials.
